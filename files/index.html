Start Earning

Our 20% applies to the rental income you receive from bookings — nothing else. Not the cleaning fee, not repairs, not photography. Here is exactly where every cost sits.

Covered by our 20%
  • Building your listing, or rebuilding an existing one — copy, SEO, photography direction, pricing setup
  • Every guest message, enquiry, review and check-in, handled by us
  • Dynamic pricing and calendar management
  • Booking and supervising cleaners, tradespeople and inspections
  • Monthly revenue reporting and a clear invoice
Paid by guests, not you
  • Cleaning fee, charged per stay through Airbnb: 375 MAD for a studio or one-bedroom, 400 MAD for two bedrooms or a riad
  • It pays the cleaner and replaces the guest items used up during the stay — toilet paper, toiletries, tea and coffee, bin bags
  • Airbnb collects it directly, so it never forms part of the income we take commission on
Billed separately
  • Professional photography where the property needs it — a one-off, passed on at the photographer’s price with nothing added. We agree the cost with you before booking anyone
  • Repairs and maintenance at the tradesperson’s price, again with nothing added. Urgent work we act on immediately up to the limit set in your management contract; everything else we clear with you first
  • Optional monthly restocking of the household supplies that are not per-stay items — cleaning products, laundry detergent, kitchen basics. Our own flat fee: 350, 450 or 600 MAD by property size. It covers different items from the cleaning fee, so nothing is charged twice

★ We work as co-hosts on your Airbnb listing. We take on listings at any rating, including ones that are underperforming — an owner with problems worth fixing is often the best person to work with. What we look at is the property itself and whether you are open to changing what is not working.

Our Story

A working host and a Marrakchi on the ground.

Caleb runs his own Airbnb in Perth and holds Superhost status on it. Your listing gets built the same way he built his own — the copy, the pricing, the guest messaging — and he handles all of it, plus your monthly reporting. He splits his year roughly evenly between Perth and Marrakech, so he is on the ground here for a good part of it. Hajar is from Marrakech and does the part that cannot be done remotely: property walkthroughs, key handovers, cleaners, tradespeople, and dealing with the local paperwork that comes with letting a property here legally. Atlas Host Co is registered in Morocco and invoices properly. Between Perth and Marrakech, one of us is awake for your guests almost around the clock.
